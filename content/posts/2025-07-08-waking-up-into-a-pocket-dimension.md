<p>Join me at 30,000 feet, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6I02UdoT6w">put your noise cancelling headphones on</a>, there’s no screen, no wifi, and they’ve served you a hypoallergenic ginger cookie and water in a thin plastic cup. You’ve emptied them and they took those away, but now you have one small napkin sitting askew in the tray dimple. You’re bored of your book. Close your eyes as the light turbulence sieves your subconscious to the surface.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!15Kj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F145f455a-abfc-429f-9400-26134c2b2d30_6774x4492.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>On the airplane a couple years ago I fell asleep, as I often do, staring out the window listening to quiet music. When I wake up I have nowhere to go, no screen to watch, no tasks to achieve, and no thoughts to mull have been remembered yet.</p>
<p>With a nap-fresh mind I could observe the humans around me and, thanks to recent training in meditation, “choose not to suffer” when they chose to cry loudly or look ugly or remind me of someone pretty. If I observed the suffering, without hoping it would end, I would be free of it.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gn_C!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbb62ec56-40ac-414a-950a-78ce1510e752_6774x4492.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>But then something else special happened as I flew more and more. Not only did I stop being averse to negative events, I realized the negativity from a crying baby was all biologically emplaced. </p>
<p><em>Of course</em> I evolved to have such a reaction to a crying baby, it would make sense for my programming to work that way. </p>
<p><em>Of course</em> I think I am a self, as I would never survive or reproduce if my constituent parts didn’t have unity of purpose. </p>
<p><em>Of course</em> I have verbal consciousness, my ancestors’ minds helped them compete for survival of their genes, which required language and narrativization of the self as one entity.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PTb4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6bf75b6a-cb30-4262-9a7b-21658cc91d7b_6774x4492.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>As I accumulated a grievously large carbon footprint that year, 2023, these thoughts continued to develop. It felt like I became closer to realizing something.</p>
<p>Everything you see and feel and remember has an associated reaction, a habit burned into your neurons. If you can slide the knife of observation between the two, you can free yourself <strong>momentarily</strong> from negative feelings, and allow positive ones to grow without becoming attached to them.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!a6TH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffb7a3683-7fc1-412b-8b57-afbf21dd8cec_4492x6774.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>On one particular flight it finally seemed to happen, but at a greater level. I realized something, and then it was gone. I experienced a feeling of non-self. It’s not important that I describe it here, but what I want to emphasize is that I’ve past it now.</p>
<p>Even today, two years later, I am still unsure of what to do about the realization. I could go about chasing it forever, striving to meditate more and more, take more flights or mess around with dream states. Or I could find in the memory of it some seed of understanding and use it to change the way I live. Either way, I have made no progress since. I’m the same man, living the same life. Maybe a bit differently, for a few months after I would look at clouds for longer, but I’m still in the major ways, the same.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wxaA!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd4e56c64-b30a-4cf4-80c8-6a701b0e4bc8_6572x4358.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>Every time I get a new grain of understanding it feels like life will get clearer and clearer, easier and easier, and that I will be richer because of it. However, this does not seem to be the case. I don’t think this knowledge is cursed, but I do feel that I would be better off not unironically saying “we live in a society” under my breath every few hours after observing something as tritely aggravating as a billboard advertisement. And maybe, just maybe, letting go of the self for longer periods of time will help me realize what I want to, but I’m really not willing to take the easy, chemical route there either. In any case, it probably won’t help me figure out what to do tomorrow.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Czog!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd790c493-ef5c-481c-8195-7d8dd0f562f7_4492x6774.jpeg" alt=""></figure>
<p>Because of this, I feel as though there is no “ultimate”. No ultimate knowledge, ideology, way of life, religion, diet, behavior, morality, or habit structure.</p>
<p>The further I go into trying to understand the world, accumulating mental models of causation, the further I feel from knowing what to actually do about it.</p>
<p>If you find the way home, let me know.</p>
<p>A future post will be about that moment in more detail. For now, enjoy those film photos from the plane, and maybe try to take some naps.</p>
