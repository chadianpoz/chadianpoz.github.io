<p>The american system of culture is composed of its monetary and financial structures, its spirit of growth, of consumerism, and of individuality, its emphasis on the act of voting and the enforcement of voting as the means to express the individual, and in journalism which can criticise the government and sometimes corporations. <strong>It is as close to a stable state, a final cultural form, as the world has ever realized<a href="#footnote-1">1</a>. It is not good or bad by any ultimate metric, but it is best at spreading and persisting in the minds and the bodies of human beings across the world.</strong></p>
<p>To understand this in the context of cultural evolution<a href="#footnote-2">2</a> I will first build a small model of human behavior. The human individual has hardware encoded into its brain and its body telling it to reproduce, survive, and socialize. It has firmware, behavior which is harder to change and is subconscious, perhaps how you treat authority or the language you dream in. It also has software, this can be changed and is changed often, and this can operate at the conscious or unconscious level, think the habits you have this year, the jokes you started making recently, or the way you interact with the supermarket. At a higher level, all humans interact and their software changes among them, where many humans have similar though not identical programs which communicate and change in waves across a society. This information transmission and the change of the software running on the mind of a population is covered by memetics, or the study of the spread of ideas.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Lsfg!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa090741f-c190-47ad-ae4c-d4227818081f_686x538.png" alt="The history of container shipping"><figcaption>SS Ideal X, the first successful container ship, 1956</figcaption></figure>
<p>In order to stay in power, american politicians are incentivised to take money from powerful corporations whose interests or resources are often in foreign lands. To achieve the liberation of those resources (not the peoples) in those nations from communist or authoritarian control is often the intent, to make business more lucrative. In addition, the overall geopolitical alignment of nations against the USSR was a key driver of actions taken abroad. Think of this as the viral vector which the West uses to spread consumerism and free market capitalism across the world. Oil is another driver of this behavior, arguably the most important. Petrodollar exchange and the oil trade is fundamentally more crucial than any other system at the disposal of Americans<a href="#footnote-3">3</a>.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!njwF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd2c9cec6-f5a3-4c06-8e6c-97ab583a6afd_2000x1250.jpeg" alt="Ghawar Oil Field"><figcaption>The most important place on Earth you’ve never heard of, Ghawar Oil Field in Saudi Arabia</figcaption></figure>
<p>The global cultural hegemony of the US is an end point of cultural optimization of the human society realized through the spread of western capitalist ideals beginning with the Renaissance’s individualism and followed by industrialization, the invention of the limited liability corporation stocks, stock trade, the birth of the global trade networks and mercantilism, the global financial system of Bretton Woods, human rights, advertising psychology, the rise of individual consumer credit, air travel, containerization, plastics, women’s rights to work and hold bank accounts, and computer networks. The apex culture has arrived by the 1990s and the Soviet attempt to push against this was largely defeated by raw free market oligarchy and immediate adoption of consumer trends. </p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!m8s_!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff122563b-b140-4a09-8a26-ed740f30ea42_432x310.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption>Giving women bank accounts and their own income effectively doubles profits, so corporations were okay with their liberation.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Consumerism spreads, it is highly potent. It is coveted. It expresses itself to the poor and inspires them, it gives America in Europe the seal of great admiration and aspiration. Dreams are only bought and sold in the West; there are no competitive dreams handed out by other ideologies. Communism handed out a dream of the individual in the context of a society, but this is not a dream, it is just a narrative. A dream in the sense that I mean here must be composed of a selfish desire. It is where one wants to see oneself. </p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!cPI9!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd2ccc09f-0e5d-45f3-998e-2767be5eb6e3_1046x798.png" alt="Torches of Freedom"><figcaption>How does this make you(r body) feel?</figcaption></figure>
<p>It is <em>completely</em> <strong>PHYSIOLOGICAL</strong> how the consumerist world appeals to the poor second or third worlder: sex, abundance, comfort, security, dignity, being accepted, and having the promise of growth or of something to look forward to. The individual is elevated to the role of king in their own life. The very opportunity to spend money on your own desires not those of your family is an expression of self love and self realization. Yet these desires are not of your imagination but ones designed by narratives of what could be possible as written by the advertisers to sell you the very things they are programming you to desire. </p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N7Cj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6b824cb3-0a62-46f0-93cf-34b829d20c15_500x375.jpeg" alt="Pirated CDs and DVDs destroyed in Morocco"><figcaption>Millions of dollars of pirated western media, DVDs, CDs, and now of course downloaded digital media is spread throughout the world each year.</figcaption></figure>
<p>This began in the 20th century and mass and telecoms let us broadcast it to the whole planet in pirated VHS or Beatles on the radio as the global system reinforced itself and settled into more entrenched optima, stable ways of organizing people and their thoughts emerged. They were not decided upon by an all powerful government program but selected for in the free market of evolutionary selection of quote systems UN quote. These could be the narratives of technologies, of nations, of companies, of genres of film or music, of financial instruments, of trade deals, of alliances, of rivalries, of fields of science, of industrial complexes parentheses military, pharma, automotive parentheses. <strong>Sticky, persistent cultural elements and ways of living emerge not due to an all powerful individual or council but due to the selection effects which are so powerful in this fast moving competitive information rich low shelter and most of all money dominated global system.</strong> </p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!WAEJ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbb7556cb-330c-4163-806c-f44c1d9483f3_500x377.jpeg" alt="Founding fathers of Europe"><figcaption>The founders of the European experiment, a vast industrial, capitalist, socialist, consumerist, federation of leaders which all speak different languages. <p>There is a clear information currency of survival, reproduction, and persistence, and that is in money. The more nebulous one is in power, but in any case money underpins the system allowing it to evolve in an extremely efficient way and allowing the ecosystem, so to speak, to be highly efficient in its selection of more competitive and more persistent systems. <strong>This is perhaps the only novel idea of the post, that the 20th century was a time of exceptional change in how cultural evolution occurred<a href="#footnote-4">4</a>. Telecommunications, air travel, and containerization, along with the rise of computing allowed for this fertile breeding ground for intense and rigorous mutation and selection of optimal social states.</strong> </p>

<p>The culture which emerged at the end of the Cold War was the most virulent and that which was best adapted to survival at massive scale. Some smaller cultures continue to exist locked in states like North Korea and Iran which resist western behavior through isolationism. Africa is also a point of interest given its recalcitrant resistance to “advancement” in the western sense. China is a key example of a blended system where authoritarianism allowed the most powerful aspects of capitalism to spread into their domain, but maintained enough control to craft a crossover culture, one which they argue is superior compared to the politically unguided and unconscious sleepwalking American experiment.</p>
<figure><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!eb5S!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd2339216-ea50-4ea6-8c10-108d5cb426e3_615x409.jpeg" alt="Easy Everything internet cafe"><figcaption>Back when we had internet cafes you could just share your favorite blogs by talking to the person next to you.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Somehow the effects of the Internet and the media finally amplified a voice and set of voices globally which opposed many aspects of Western culture, the populist voices. Do not be shocked when the global order shifts as the order will only shift in ways within the consumerist framework. Adjustments to the global order are only to make it more persistent. Key aspects will never change while others will adapt to changes in scale and the technological landscape that the primate human beings operate in. While anti interventionism and isolationism were parts of the goals of Trump and Brexiteers respectively they have failed in practical measures due to the interests of global business and the market fighting back against them. The banks are powerful. We the people are not. </p>
<p>“History is determined by those who are determined to act in unison” as <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AEPSUC-UQ5k">Professor Jiang</a> notes, and since we are now in an individualist culture, the literal or social martyrdom needed to upheave society and unseat consumerism will simply not arise<a href="#footnote-5">5</a>. Consumerism has grabbed us by the brain and it won’t let us go. It is too optimized to please us on short, powerful timescales to make revolution appealing. Then we must ask, do we even want revolution? What would it look like? Can we come up with a suitably well adapted replacement ideology or way of life? If consumerism has defeated all others, how can we imagine it won’t just come back from the ashes even if another order rules the average person’s psyche for a few decades?</p>
<p>What is best for us humans as a species? What is best for our nation? What is best for our community? and most importantly, What is best for <em>me</em>?</p>
