<blockquote><p>Say, I will become overwhelmed by illness and die.</p><p>Title my life with tragic lies, I tell to none other than my own narrator. He gushes with pride, racked with guilt despite it. Forgetful and jealous the same.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>By, at this moment comprehending instantaneous scientific enlightenment, wisdom of vast spaces, knowledge of orders and mechanics, I encompass some fallacy of the human experience. The singular feeling of epiphany trapped still and forever in the metabolic plans allowed in the skull.  No more ultimate can a moment be than that ordained by an experiencing mind. Desert this way, and recess with the anyways.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>We look upon the beauteous natural order in the savanna, jaws of agile bound upon the striped creature in a terrible red crown. Bitten by this optimism we can even see the human wars from the deck of the <em>Beagle</em>, with no muddled animal anxiety aboard.</p><p>If only the zebra could strike such a high repose, mollified, and eyes cast nadir to everything placed by time’s spatial throes.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>In equal contemplation, man respects the math that strikes up horror, but descends to sulk as viscera and bone tend to do. </p><p>Perhaps only so many matches can be lighted, and so the heat and smoke of brave reception twist away into night.</p></blockquote>
