We built the first tool capable of at-home, lab-grade multi-compound chemical analysis.

Otea operates autonomously, allowing everyday people to provide non-invasive samples within their own home.

It has the same fundamental goal as state-of-the-art pharmaceutical analysis systems like liquid chromatography: separate compounds in a complex sample and measure each one precisely. Our coefficients of variation are within 3% of industry-standard commercial instruments, but our unit costs us 100x less to build and operate.

Otea is a conjugation of the Spanish verb *otear*, "to view from a distance". Its native autonomy springs from NASA technology developments over two decades to operate robotically on Mars and Europa.

Otea now provides a compressed representation of the output of the human metabolic system.

Deleon's goal is to precisely map behaviors, diet, drugs, and other interventions to metabolic pathway activations.

*The [original post](https://deleon.bio/article?slug=2026-10-01-otea-daily-metabolomics) has an interactive figure: each day's activity, such as a hard workout, speeds up or slows down pathways on the KEGG global metabolic map, and each bar shows the share of reactions near an Otea amino acid that changed.*

Otea does not aim to quantify individual diagnostic compounds, but rather to trace, in urine, patterns of the metabolic pathways being activated upstream in harder to access blood and tissue.

By combining daily biobanked blood data with daily Otea urine results, we create a mapping between metabolism and urine metabolites. This way, we connect the rich though invasive viewpoint from blood sampling, learn what metabolic pathways are being activated, and see how this is represented in the more compressed space of the urinary amino acid fingerprint.

The kidneys filter blood and pass a small part of it into urine. From paired daily samples, our neural network learns the same compression, from ~10,000 blood features to 10 amino acids.

We are continuously augmenting Otea, so our ability to see new compounds decompresses this space and increases our sensitivity to small metabolic changes.

Humans should have constant feedback on what behaviors and interventions are best for them as individuals. This is made possible by daily sampling. Otea plus our AI models will be the first to enable this frontier.

Only with Otea can we gain this insight every single day, at home.
