## Experience

**Deleon Technologies, Inc.** · Co-founder and CEO · Atlanta, GA · 2025 to present

- Founded and lead an AI research company that measures human biochemistry every day, inside living people, to train metabolic foundation models. Team spans biology, chemistry, ML, software, electrical, and aerospace engineering.
- Built *Exploreon*, an experiment design model that reached the same final accuracy as Claude Opus 5.5 and Monte Carlo sampling with 7x and 12x less data in simulated studies.
- Built *Juniper*, an agentic instrument control loop that interprets each experiment, considers the literature and settings tried before, and proposes the next run.
- Built personal time-series ODE models of each participant's daily biochemistry that update with every new sample.
- Built the company's modeling stack: metabolic flux models, time-series models, neural networks for chemical peak identification, Pareto-frontier optimization, and an LLM evaluation harness with a held-out test set on real lab data.
- Designed and ran a longitudinal daily dried urine spot cohort with at-home collection, daily surveys, and wearable data.
- Built the Otea, a portable CE-LIF biomolecule reader with disposable plastic microfluidic cartridges, at about 1% of the cost of commercial systems.
- PI on an NSF SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research) Phase I award.

**Georgia Institute of Technology, Stockton Lab** · PhD Researcher · Atlanta, GA · 2021 to 2025

- Dissertation: *[Molecular Biosignature Detection in Hypersaline Planetary Analogs](/thesis/)*, for the NASA Oceans Across Space and Time consortium.
- Led field sampling at the Orca Basin (R/V Point Sur, 2023), Western Australia transient lakes (2022), and Valles Caldera (2021).
- Developed an astronaut urinary radiation biodosimetry panel with Chris Mason (Weill Cornell Medicine), validated on crew samples.
- Invented a submersible water activity meter for hypersaline planetary exploration.

**NASA Goddard Space Flight Center** · Researcher · Greenbelt, MD · 2019 to 2021

- Planetary Environments Lab: searched for organic signs of life on Mars with the Curiosity rover's Sample Analysis at Mars (SAM) team. Optimized the SAM wet chemistry experiment for Glen Torridon, Gale crater.
- Astrobiology Analytical Laboratory: measured amino acids in the Asuka 12236 meteorite and 1-billion-year-old subterranean water. Developed a desalting procedure for low-biomass samples.

**Georgetown University, NASA Laboratory for Agnostic Biosignatures** · Researcher · Washington, DC · 2020 to 2021

- Worked on how to recognize life without assuming Earth-like biochemistry, using chemical complexity as an agnostic biosignature. Co-designed a meta-analysis of null life detection results.

**Penn State, House Research Group** · Undergraduate Researcher · 2018 to 2019

- Prebiotic organic phosphorylation chemistry by phosphorus-31 NMR.
